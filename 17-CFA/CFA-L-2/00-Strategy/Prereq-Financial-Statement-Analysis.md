# Prerequisite refresher: Financial Statement Analysis

The 2026 Level II curriculum ships this Level I reader (310 pages) as assumed knowledge; it is not examined directly at Level II, but the modules below assume it without re-explaining it.
Read the listed sections only when the Level II note flags a gap; do not study the reader front to back.

| Reader module | What Level II assumes from it | Level II modules that lean on it |
|---|---|---|
| LM1 Introduction to Financial Reporting | IFRS framework, elements, OCI | FSA LM3 (translation to OCI), FSA LM5 (quality) |
| LM2 Income Statements | revenue recognition, EPS basics, non-recurring items | FSA LM5, Corporate Issuers LM1 (EPS after repurchase), Equity LM4 (normalized EPS) |
| LM3 Balance Sheets | classification, book value, marketable securities categories | FSA LM1 (investment classification), FSA LM4 (bank balance sheets), Equity LM5 (book value for RI) |
| LM4 Cash Flow Statements | CFO under both standards, indirect method, FCFF and FCFE from CFO | Equity LM3 (free cash flow), FSA LM5 (cash flow quality), FSA LM6 |
| LM5 Inventories | LIFO vs FIFO effects, LIFO reserve | FSA LM5, FSA LM6 (adjustments for comparability) |
| LM6 Long-Lived Assets | capitalizing vs expensing, impairment, revaluation model | FSA LM1 (goodwill and impairment), FSA LM5 |
| LM7 Income Taxes | deferred tax assets and liabilities, effective tax rate reconciliation | FSA LM2 (pension deferred taxes), FSA LM5, Corporate Issuers LM1 (imputation) |
| LM8 Non-Current Liabilities | bond amortization, leases, pension basics | FSA LM2 (post-employment), FSA LM4 (bank liabilities), Fixed Income LM4 |
| LM9 Applications of Financial Statement Analysis | ratio framework, DuPont, forecasting | FSA LM6 (integration framework), Equity LM1, FSA LM4 (CAMELS ratios) |

Where to find it: `cfa-program2026L2PrereqRead-Financial Statement Analysis.pdf` in the curriculum PDF folder.
