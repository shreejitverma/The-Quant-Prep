# Prerequisite refresher: Quantitative Methods

The 2026 Level II curriculum ships this Level I reader (340 pages) as assumed knowledge; it is not examined directly at Level II, but the modules below assume it without re-explaining it.
Read the listed sections only when the Level II note flags a gap; do not study the reader front to back.

| Reader module | What Level II assumes from it | Level II modules that lean on it |
|---|---|---|
| LM1 Interest Rates, Present Value, and Future Value | TVM mechanics, effective vs stated rates, annuity and perpetuity PVs, cash flow additivity | Equity LM2-LM3 (DDM, FCF), Fixed Income LM1-LM2 (spot and forward discounting), Derivatives LM1 (carry pricing), Corporate Issuers LM3 |
| LM2 Organizing, Visualizing, and Describing Data | means, variances, covariance and correlation, skewness and kurtosis | Quant LM1-LM2 (regression output), PM LM5 (VaR inputs), PM LM6 (backtesting statistics) |
| LM3 Probability Concepts | conditional probability, expected value and variance of a portfolio, Bayes | Quant LM6 (ML classification), Fixed Income LM4 (default probability trees), PM LM1 |
| LM4 Common Probability Distributions | normal and lognormal, t and chi-square, binomial, Monte Carlo basics | Quant LM2 (t and F critical values), Derivatives LM2 (binomial and BSM), PM LM5 (parametric VaR), PM LM6 (simulation) |
| LM5 Sampling and Estimation | standard error, confidence intervals, sampling bias | Quant LM2 (prediction intervals), Quant LM7 (big data sampling), PM LM6 (data snooping) |
| LM6 Basics of Hypothesis Testing | null and alternative, one and two tailed tests, p-values, Type I and II errors | Quant LM1-LM5 (every coefficient and model test), PM LM2 (information coefficient significance) |

Where to find it: `cfa-program2026L2PrereqRead-Quantitative Methods.pdf` in the curriculum PDF folder.
