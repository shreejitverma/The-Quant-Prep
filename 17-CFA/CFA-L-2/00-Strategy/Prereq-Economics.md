# Prerequisite refresher: Economics

The 2026 Level II curriculum ships this Level I reader (242 pages) as assumed knowledge; it is not examined directly at Level II, but the modules below assume it without re-explaining it.
Read the listed sections only when the Level II note flags a gap; do not study the reader front to back.

| Reader module | What Level II assumes from it | Level II modules that lean on it |
|---|---|---|
| LM1 Topics in Demand and Supply Analysis | elasticities, marginal cost curves | Economics LM2 (growth inputs), Corporate Issuers LM4 |
| LM2 Introduction to the Firm and Market Organization | market structures, pricing power | Equity LM1 (industry analysis), Corporate Issuers LM4 (restructuring rationale) |
| LM3 Aggregate Output, Prices, and Economic Growth | GDP accounting, production function, productivity | Economics LM2 (growth accounting, Cobb-Douglas), PM LM1 |
| LM4 Introduction to Business Cycles | cycle phases, indicators | PM LM1 (business cycle and asset returns), Fixed Income LM1 (yield curve and cycle) |
| LM5 Monetary and Fiscal Policy | policy tools, Taylor rule, fiscal multipliers | Economics LM1 (policy effects on FX), PM LM1, Fixed Income LM1 |
| LM6 International Trade and Capital Flows | balance of payments accounts | Economics LM1 (BOP flows and exchange rates) |
| LM7 Currency Exchange Rates | quotes, cross rates, forward premium basics | Economics LM1 (bid-offer, triangular arbitrage, parity conditions), Derivatives LM1 (currency forwards) |
