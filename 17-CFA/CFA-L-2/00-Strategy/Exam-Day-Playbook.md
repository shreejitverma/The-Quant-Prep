# Exam Day Playbook: 24 November 2026

## Format

- Two sessions of 2 hours 12 minutes with an optional break between them.
- 11 item sets per session, 4 questions each, 44 questions per session; 20 of the 22 sets are scored and 2 are unscored trials that you cannot identify, so treat every set as scored.
- Confirm the current session structure and permitted calculators on the CFA Institute candidate page the week before the exam; this playbook reflects the 2026 curriculum front matter and the CFA Institute Level II page as of 26 August 2026.

## The week before

- No new material after 20 November.
- Daily: Slides formula decks, calculator drill, SRS review, one Ethics reread of the Standards table in [[17-CFA/CFA-L-2/10-Ethics/LM01-Code-of-Ethics-and-Standards-of-Professional-Conduct|LM1]].
- Two full nights of normal sleep before the exam are worth more than any additional item set.
- Check the test center route, the ID requirements, and that the BA II Plus has fresh batteries and a backup.

## The morning

- Calculator reset: 2ND, RESET, ENTER; then set P/Y = 1 (2ND, P/Y, 1, ENTER), C/Y = 1, decimals to 9 (2ND, FORMAT, 9, ENTER), and END mode; confirm no BGN indicator.
- Eat as on normal study days; nothing new.
- Arrive 45 minutes early; late arrivals may be refused.

## Pacing inside a session

| Checkpoint | Time elapsed | Item sets done |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 36 min | 2 |
| 2 | 72 min | 4 |
| 3 | 108 min | 6 |
| 4 | 132 min (end) | 11 |

The first checkpoints are deliberately slow because early sets set the rhythm; the last hour must hold 5 sets, so if checkpoint 2 is behind, switch to answering qualitative questions first in every remaining set.

## Per item set

1. Read the question stems first (30 seconds); note which are calculate and which are describe.
2. Read the vignette once, writing the input list (numbers with units and dates) on the scratch sheet.
3. Answer describe and identify questions first, then calculate questions.
4. For each calculate question, before selecting, name the error that would produce each rejected option; if you cannot name one, recheck your sign and period conventions.
5. Flag and move on at 5 minutes on any single question; no blanks.

## Between sessions

- Do not discuss questions; do not reopen notes.
- Eat, walk, reset the calculator mode check, and sit for the second session with the same pacing table.

## Failure modes and their rules

| Failure mode | Rule |
|---|---|
| Calculator in BGN mode | Reset before each session; check the indicator after any TVM problem with an annuity due |
| Semiannual vs annual | Write the period count and the period rate on the scratch sheet before pressing anything |
| Reading the wrong analyst's statement | Underline the name in the stem, then find that name in the vignette |
| Anchoring to the first plausible option | Compute first, then look at the options |
| Spending 8 minutes on one question | Flag at 5 minutes; the marginal question is worth the same as any other |
