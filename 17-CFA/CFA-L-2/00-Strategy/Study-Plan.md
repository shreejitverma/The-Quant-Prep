# Study Plan: 90 days to 24 November 2026

Three overlapping passes.
Hours assumed: 3.5 on weekdays, 7.0 on weekend days, about 406 in total; edit the properties above and regenerate with `python3 17-CFA/scripts/cfa_l2/build_plan.py` if your availability changes.

From week 3: one interleaved item set per day from already-studied modules.
