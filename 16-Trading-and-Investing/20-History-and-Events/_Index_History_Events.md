# History and Event Case Files Pillar Index

The History and Event Case Files pillar provides forensic autopsies of landmark financial panics, structural flash crashes, and liquidity crises across two centuries of market history.
Rather than treating market shocks as unpredictable black swans, this curriculum decomposes the deterministic feedback loops, excessive leverage structures, illiquid routing bottlenecks, and regulatory failures that repeatedly trigger market breakdowns.

---

## Learning Objectives

- Deconstruct the systemic transmission mechanisms of market liquidity freezing, margin spiraling, and clearinghouse collateral calls.
- Analyze the microstructural dynamics of algorithmic feedback loops in the 1987 Black Monday crash, the 2010 Flash Crash, and the 2021 meme stock squeeze.
- Evaluate the vulnerability of highly leveraged non-linear derivative structures during the 1998 LTCM collapse and the 2022 UK LDI pension crisis.
- Quantify negative-price phenomena and commodity expiration squeezes during physical storage saturation events.
- Formulate institutional pre-mortem risk protocols to protect proprietary and fund capital against sudden gap risk, peg breaks, and exchange halts.

## The Ten Historical Crisis Case Files

1. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/01-1929-Wall-Street-Crash-and-Great-Depression|01. 1929 Wall Street Crash & Great Depression]]
   - Margin loans at 10% down, ticker tape lag, and nationwide bank run contagion.
2. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/02-1987-Black-Monday-and-Portfolio-Insurance|02. 1987 Black Monday Portfolio Insurance Collapse]]
   - Mechanical dynamic hedging sell algorithms, NYSE DOT queue congestion, and the single-day 20.5% index plunge.
3. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/03-1998-LTCM-Collapse-and-Relative-Value-Arbitrage|03. 1998 LTCM Collapse & Relative Value Arbitrage]]
   - Russian sovereign default, flight to on-the-run Treasuries, swap spread blowout, and the Fed-orchestrated $3.6B bailout.
4. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/04-2000-Dot-Com-Bubble-and-Telecom-Crash|04. 2000 Dot-Com Bubble Collapse & Telecom Implosion]]
   - Triple-digit price-to-sales multiples, venture capital IPO lockup expirations, and the multi-year 78% NASDAQ drawdown.
5. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/05-2008-Global-Financial-Crisis-and-Lehman-Collapse|05. 2008 Global Financial Crisis & Lehman Brothers Collapse]]
   - Subprime mortgage CDO securitization, run on tri-party repo, Reserve Primary Fund breaking the buck, and systemic credit seizure.
6. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/06-2010-Flash-Crash-and-LOB-Evaporation|06. 2010 Flash Crash & Algorithmic Liquidity Evaporation]]
   - Waddell & Reed automated E-mini sell order, high-frequency quoting withdrawal, cross-market arbitrage transmission, and stub quotes.
7. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/07-2015-Swiss-Franc-Unpeg-and-Broker-Insolvency|07. 2015 Swiss Franc (EUR/CHF) Peg Abandonment]]
   - Sudden SNB policy reversal, 30% intraday currency repricing, complete execution void, and retail broker balance liquidations.
8. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/08-2020-COVID-Crash-and-Negative-WTI-Crude|08. 2020 COVID Crash & Negative WTI Crude Oil Expiration]]
   - Lockdown demand destruction, Cushing storage exhaustion, retail ETF roll distortion, and physical delivery settlement at -$37.63/bbl.
9. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/09-2021-GameStop-Meme-Squeeze-and-DTCC-Margin-Halt|09. 2021 GameStop (GME) Short Squeeze & DTCC Margin Halt]]
   - Social media call option buying, market maker gamma reflexivity, short interest > 140%, and clearinghouse emergency collateral calls.
10. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/20-History-and-Events/10-2022-UK-LDI-Pension-Crisis-and-Gilt-Spiral|10. 2022 UK Gilt Crisis & LDI Pension Fund Margin Spiral]]
    - Unfunded mini-budget fiscal shock, 30-year Gilt yield spike of 150 bps in 4 days, leveraged swap margin calls, and Bank of England intervention.
