# Fundamental Analysis and Corporate Forensics Index

The Fundamental Analysis and Corporate Forensics curriculum provides the empirical, accounting, and strategic foundations required to evaluate corporate earning power and detect accounting manipulation.
It equips quantitative researchers and fundamental investors with the analytical rigor to audit three-statement mechanics, dissect capital productivity, isolate discretionary accruals, and identify corporate distress from regulatory filings.

---

## Learning Objectives

- Master the circular mathematics linking the Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement.
- Measure working capital efficiency, cash conversion cycles, and negative working capital float dynamics.
- Distinguish maintenance capital expenditures from growth reinvestment to calculate true Owner Earnings.
- Deconstruct Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) into operating margin and capital turnover, and execute 5-stage DuPont analyses.
- Isolate discretionary accounting accruals using Sloan's Accrual Anomaly and the Modified Jones Model.
- Compute the Beneish M-Score and Piotroski F-Score to screen out accounting manipulators and value traps.
- Audit industry competitive structures and economic moats using Porter's Five Forces and Helmer's 7 Powers.
- Execute exhaustive footnote forensic audits across SEC Form 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, and DEF 14A proxy statements.

## Fundamental Analysis Curriculum Map

The fundamental analysis track comprises eight comprehensive concept notes containing 256 verified practice questions and problem cards:

1. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/01-Three-Statement-Linkages-and-Cash-Flow-Dynamics|01. Three-Statement Linkages and Cash Flow Dynamics]]
   - The circular mechanics of the three financial statements.
   - Operating Cash Flow (CFO), Free Cash Flow to Firm (FCFF), and Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE).
   - The economic reality of Stock-Based Compensation (SBC) as real shareholder dilution.
   - Deferred Tax Assets (DTAs), Deferred Tax Liabilities (DTLs), and valuation allowances.
   - Reconciling Net Income to physical cash generation.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-001` through `mkt-fa-032`.

2. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/02-Working-Capital-Cycles-and-Cash-Conversion|02. Working Capital Cycles and Cash Conversion]]
   - Operating working capital components: Days Sales Outstanding (DSO), Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO), and Days Payable Outstanding (DPO).
   - Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC) formulation and economic interpretation.
   - Negative working capital as interest-free customer float (Amazon, Dell, Costco).
   - Forensic red flags: aggressive channel stuffing, supplier stretches, and inventory build-up.
   - Reconciling working capital swings to free cash flow.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-033` through `mkt-fa-064`.

3. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/03-Capex-Maintenance-vs-Growth-and-Owner-Earnings|03. Capex Maintenance vs Growth and Owner Earnings]]
   - Warren Buffett's Owner Earnings formulation and economic significance.
   - Separating maintenance capex (depreciation replacement) from discretionary growth capex.
   - Five quantitative methods to estimate maintenance capex: depreciation ratio, asset turnover, growth-adjusted formula, unit economics, and management disclosure.
   - Capital intensity ratios and return on incremental invested capital.
   - Free cash flow distortions under high inflation and asset aging.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-065` through `mkt-fa-096`.

4. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/04-ROIC-Decomposition-and-DuPont-Analysis|04. ROIC Decomposition and DuPont Analysis]]
   - Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) vs Return on Equity (ROE) and Return on Assets (ROA).
   - Operating approach vs financing approach to Invested Capital.
   - NOPAT margin (pricing power) vs capital turnover (asset velocity).
   - 3-stage and 5-stage DuPont decompositions isolating tax burden, interest burden, and operating efficiency.
   - Economic Value Added (EVA) and Return on Incremental Invested Capital (ROIIC).
   - Balance sheet adjustments: operating leases (ASC 842), R&D capitalization, and excess cash.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-097` through `mkt-fa-128`.

5. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/05-Forensic-Accounting-Sloan-Accruals-and-Earnings-Quality|05. Forensic Accounting, Sloan Accruals, and Earnings Quality]]
   - Richard Sloan's (1996) Accrual Anomaly: cash flow persistence vs accrual mean reversion.
   - Balance sheet accruals vs cash flow statement accruals scaled by total assets.
   - Decomposing normal from discretionary accruals via the Modified Jones Model.
   - Classic financial shenanigans: premature revenue, expense deferral, and cookie-jar reserves.
   - Systematic diagnostic thresholds and quantitative short candidate identification.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-129` through `mkt-fa-160`.

6. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/06-Fraud-Detection-Beneish-M-Score-and-Piotroski-F-Score|06. Fraud Detection, Beneish M-Score, and Piotroski F-Score]]
   - The Beneish M-Score 8-variable probabilistic model for earnings manipulation.
   - Interpretation of DSRI, GMI, AQI, SGI, DEPI, SGAI, LVGI, and TATA indices.
   - The -1.78 threshold and the historical identification of Enron.
   - Joseph Piotroski's 9-point F-Score fundamental momentum framework.
   - Profitability, leverage/liquidity, and operating efficiency binary scoring.
   - Integrating M-Score and F-Score to screen value winners and short value traps.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-161` through `mkt-fa-192`.

7. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/07-Industry-Competitive-Dynamics-and-Porter-Helmer|07. Industry Competitive Dynamics, Porter's Five Forces, and Helmer's 7 Powers]]
   - Structural industry profitability vs company-specific execution.
   - Michael Porter's Five Forces: entrants, buyers, suppliers, substitutes, and rivalry.
   - Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers: Scale Economies, Network Effects, Counter-Positioning, Switching Costs, Branding, Cornered Resource, and Process Power.
   - Competitive Advantage Period (CAP) and the economic fade rate of ROIC towards WACC.
   - Winner-take-most market dynamics and economic moat sustainability.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-193` through `mkt-fa-224`.

8. [[16-Trading-and-Investing/Investing/Fundamental-Analysis/08-Financial-Filings-Forensics-10K-Footnotes-and-Red-Flags|08. Financial Filings Forensics, 10-K Footnotes, and Governance Red Flags]]
   - Statutory SEC filing architecture: Form 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14A proxy, and Form 4.
   - Footnote forensics: Revenue Recognition (ASC 606 contract assets/liabilities, RPO backlog), Income Taxes, Debt Covenants, Pensions, and Related-Party Transactions.
   - Executive compensation forensics: the "Adjusted EBITDA" bonus gaming trap and golden parachutes.
   - 8-K emergency red flag triggers: Item 4.01 (auditor changes) and Item 4.02 (accounting restatements).
   - The 20-Point Forensic Audit Checklist for enterprise risk detection.
   - Cards: `mkt-fa-225` through `mkt-fa-256`.
