# Stock: General Electric (GE)


## Investment Thesis
A cautionary tale of over-financialization and diworsification. From 'Godhood' under Jack Welch to near-collapse.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Eroded Power: Once had Scale and Branding, lost through poor capital allocation and opaque accounting.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Decades of wealth destruction after the 2000 peak.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
The 'Complexity Trap' and 'Financial Engineering' anti-models.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Munger: 'A classic example of how incentives and culture can go wrong.'
