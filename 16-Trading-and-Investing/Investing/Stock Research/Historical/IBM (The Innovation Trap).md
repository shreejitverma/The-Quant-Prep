# Stock: IBM (IBM)


## Investment Thesis
The 'Innovation Trap'. Dominated the mainframe era but failed to lead in PC, Cloud, and AI transitions despite deep R&D.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Switching Costs: Still exists in mainframes, but the moat 'bridge' didn't reach the new islands of tech.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Stagnant revenue and reliance on share buybacks for years.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Legacy tech debt; inability to attract top-tier AI talent compared to Big Tech.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Buffett: 'Even a great moat can be filled in by technology shifts.'
