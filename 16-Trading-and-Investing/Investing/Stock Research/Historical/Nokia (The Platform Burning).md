# Stock: Nokia (NOK)


## Investment Thesis
The 'Platform Burning' tale. Dominated mobile phones but was decimated by the iPhone/Android shift due to software failure.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Scale Economies: Massive in hardware, but irrelevant when the 'Game' shifted to Software Ecosystems.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Massive loss of market value; currently a telecom infrastructure company.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Ecosystem Network Effects (iOS/Android) over hardware scale.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Lynch: 'The game can change overnight.'
