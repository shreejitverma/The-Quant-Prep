# Stock: Kodak (KODK)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
The 'Digital Blindness' tale. Invented the digital camera but suppressed it to protect its high-margin film business.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Branding: Once world-class, now a ghost. Scale Economies: Lost as the world moved from chemicals to bits.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Bankruptcy in 2012; a total loss for long-term shareholders.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Creative destruction; the 'Innovator's Dilemma'.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Munger: 'Cannibalize yourself before someone else does.'
