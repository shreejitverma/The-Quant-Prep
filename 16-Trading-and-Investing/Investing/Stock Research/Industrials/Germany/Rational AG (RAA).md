# Stock: Rational AG (RAA)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
Dominant world market leader in combi-steamers for professional kitchens with >50% market share. Extreme focus on one product category.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Process Power: Decades of specialized manufacturing knowledge. Scale Economies: Dominant market share allows for highest R&D spend in the niche. Branding: The 'Gold Standard' in commercial kitchens.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Consistently high double-digit ROIC and steady revenue growth over decades.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Slowing growth in mature markets; competition from lower-cost Asian manufacturers.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Lynch would love the 'boring but dominant' nature of kitchen equipment.
