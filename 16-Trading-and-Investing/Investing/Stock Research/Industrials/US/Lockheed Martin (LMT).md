# Stock: Lockheed Martin (LMT)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Lockheed Martin is the backbone of the US and allied defense infrastructure. It is the lead contractor for the F-35 program - the most expensive and advanced fighter jet program in history. Its revenue is backed by multi-decade government contracts, providing high visibility and "sovereign" level security.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Cornered Resource:** Exclusive technology and classified patents for advanced aeronautics and missile systems.
2. **Switching Costs:** Once a nation's Air Force is built on the F-35 platform, the cost of switching to a different ecosystem (training, parts, software) is nearly infinite.
3. **Scale Economies:** Only a handful of companies on earth can manage the complexity of a fifth-generation fighter program.
4. **Network Economies:** Interoperability between allied nations using LMT systems creates a "standard" in defense.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Cold War Era:** Foundation of aerospace dominance.
- **The F-35 Era:** Securing the next 50 years of air superiority and recurring maintenance revenue.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Political Risk (Budget Cuts):** Dependency on US defense spending.
- **Fixed-Price Contracts:** Inflation eating into margins if costs are not passed on.
- **Geopolitics:** Export restrictions and diplomatic shifts.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Historically appreciated the "moat" of defense contractors, though he has rotated in and out of the sector.
- **Peter Lynch:** Would love the "captive customer" (governments) and the high barriers to entry.
