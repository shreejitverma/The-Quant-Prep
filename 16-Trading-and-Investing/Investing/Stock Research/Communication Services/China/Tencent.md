# Stock: Tencent (0700.HK)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Tencent is the ultimate "Super-App" ecosystem player through WeChat (1.3B+ users). It is the world's largest gaming company and a premier venture capital investor in global tech. In China, Tencent is the digital infrastructure of daily life - from payments to social networking to enterprise services.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Network Effects:** WeChat's social network is the most powerful "moat" in tech; everyone in China must be on it for social and professional life.
*   **Switching Costs:** Extremely high; an individual's entire digital identity, payment history, and social graph are tied to WeChat.
*   **Scale Economies:** Dominance in gaming (distribution and development) allows for massive R&D and acquisition budgets.
*   **Cornered Resource:** Deepest data pool on Chinese consumer behavior and a massive portfolio of investments (Epic Games, Supercell, Meituan, etc.).

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Growth:** Delivered incredible CAGR for two decades.
*   **Resilience:** Successfully navigated the 2021-2022 regulatory crackdown by pivoting to "Social Value" and "Enterprise Tech."

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Regulatory Risk:** Ongoing oversight by the Chinese government (CCP) regarding data privacy, gaming time, and antitrust.
*   **Geopolitics:** Potential for "delisting" or restrictions on its international investments.
*   **Macro:** Slowdown in Chinese consumer spending.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Munger:** Long-time admirer of Li Lu (who invested in China) and the "monopoly-like" power of Tencent.
*   **Buffett:** Might see it as a "digital toll bridge" - it's impossible to do business in China without crossing it.
