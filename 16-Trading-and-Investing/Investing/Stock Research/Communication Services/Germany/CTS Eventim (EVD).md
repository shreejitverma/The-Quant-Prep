# Stock: CTS Eventim (EVD)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
European ticketing and live entertainment monopoly. Vertically integrated from venues to software.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Network Effects: Promoters want the largest audience; fans go where the tickets are. Scale Economies: Dominant platform in Europe.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
High cash flow generation and dominance in the European market.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Regulatory scrutiny of ticketing fees; emergence of direct-to-fan platforms.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Buffett: 'A franchise is a business where you can raise prices without losing customers.'
