# Stock: Exxon Mobil (XOM)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Exxon Mobil is the largest US integrated oil and gas company. It is a "Supermajor" that controls the entire value chain - from exploration and production (Upstream) to refining and chemicals (Downstream). Its scale and balance sheet allow it to invest in multi-decade, massive projects (like Guyana) that smaller players cannot touch.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Massive vertical integration allows Exxon to capture margin at every stage, providing a natural hedge against oil price volatility.
2. **Cornered Resource:** Ownership of some of the lowest-cost, highest-quality oil reserves in the world (e.g., Permian Basin, Guyana).
3. **Process Power:** Decades of expertise in complex project management and chemical engineering.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **Standard Oil Legacy:** Deep roots in the American energy industry.
- **The Shale Revolution:** Aggressive expansion into US unconventional oil.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Energy Transition (Anti-Model: The Dinosaur):** Long-term shift away from fossil fuels to renewables.
- **Commodity Price Risk:** Highly sensitive to global Brent/WTI prices.
- **Environmental Litigation:** Climate change-related lawsuits and carbon taxes.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Recently made a massive bet on the sector (primarily Occidental and Chevron), citing the long-term necessity of fossil fuels despite the transition.
- **Peter Lynch:** A classic play on the "Cycle" and "Assets."
