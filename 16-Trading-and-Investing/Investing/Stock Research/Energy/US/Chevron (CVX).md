# Stock: Chevron (CVX)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Chevron is the other half of the US energy duopoly. It is known for its "capital discipline" - often returning more cash to shareholders via dividends and buybacks than its peers. It has a high-quality portfolio with significant exposure to the Permian Basin and LNG (Liquefied Natural Gas), which is seen as a "bridge fuel" for the energy transition.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Global integrated model that minimizes unit costs.
2. **Cornered Resource:** Significant positions in low-cost basins and massive LNG projects in Australia.
3. **Financial Strength:** One of the strongest balance sheets in the sector, allowing it to maintain dividends even during oil price crashes.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **Dividend Aristocrat:** Over 35 years of consecutive dividend increases.
- **Strategic M&A:** Successful integration of Noble Energy and Hess (pending).

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Stranded Assets:** Risk that some oil reserves will never be pumped due to climate regulation.
- **Project Execution:** Risks associated with massive, multi-billion dollar offshore projects.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** One of his largest holdings. He values the management's capital allocation and the world's continued reliance on oil.
- **Charlie Munger:** Respected the "essentiality" of energy to civilization.
