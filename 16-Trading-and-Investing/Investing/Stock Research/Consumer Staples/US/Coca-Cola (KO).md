# Stock: Coca-Cola (KO)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Coca-Cola is the world's most recognized brand. It sells a "moment of happiness" in a bottle. Its asset-light business model (selling syrup/concentrate to bottlers) allows for incredible returns on equity and massive cash flow generation. It is the ultimate "inflation hedge" because of its immense pricing power.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Branding:** The strongest brand in the world, allowing for a price premium over generic colas.
2. **Scale Economies:** The world's most extensive distribution system - you can find a Coke in the remotest villages on earth.
3. **Cornered Resource:** The "Secret Formula" (psychologically, if not literally).

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Goizueta Era:** Focus on shareholder value and massive international expansion.
- **The Buffett Investment (1988):** Buffett bought 7% of the company, calling it the "inevitable."

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Sugar Taxes:** Government intervention to reduce obesity.
- **Plastic Waste:** Environmental ESG concerns regarding single-use bottles.
- **Water Scarcity:** Access to clean water for production in developing nations.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** "If you gave me $100 billion and said take away the soft drink leadership of Coca-Cola in the world, I'd give it back to you and say it can't be done."
- **Charlie Munger:** Used KO as the primary example in his "How to turn $2 million into $2 trillion" speech.
