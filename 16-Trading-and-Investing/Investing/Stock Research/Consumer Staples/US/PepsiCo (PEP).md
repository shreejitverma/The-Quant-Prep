# Stock: PepsiCo (PEP)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
PepsiCo is a powerhouse in both beverages and snacks (Frito-Lay). While Coca-Cola dominates pure beverages, Pepsi's snacks business is a higher-growth, higher-margin engine. It owns the entire snack aisle (Doritos, Lay's, Cheetos), creating a "salty/sweet" flywheel that is extremely resistant to recession.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Direct Store Delivery (DSD) network is one of the largest in the world, allowing Pepsi to put products on shelves more efficiently than any newcomer.
2. **Branding:** Massive portfolio of "Billion Dollar Brands."
3. **Switching Costs:** Low at the individual level, but high for retailers who need the "Anchor" brands that Pepsi provides to drive foot traffic.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **Dividend King:** Decades of consistent dividend growth.
- **Snack Dominance:** Frito-Lay consistently contributes over half of the company's operating profit.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Health Trends:** Secular shift away from sugary drinks and processed snacks.
- **Input Costs:** Commodity price inflation (sugar, corn, aluminum, oil).

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Peter Lynch:** Would love the "consumer addiction" aspect of Frito-Lay products.
- **Warren Buffett:** Prefers KO for the brand, but has often acknowledged the operational excellence of Pepsi.
