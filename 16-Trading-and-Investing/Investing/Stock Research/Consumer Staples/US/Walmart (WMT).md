# Stock: Walmart (WMT)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Walmart is the world's largest retailer and largest private employer. Its "Everyday Low Price" (EDLP) strategy has created a massive, loyal customer base. It has successfully pivoted to omnichannel retail, using its 4,700+ US stores as fulfillment centers for e-commerce, directly challenging Amazon while maintaining dominance in physical grocery.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Unmatched purchasing power. Walmart can squeeze suppliers more than any other retailer, passing the savings to customers.
2. **Process Power:** World-class logistics and supply chain management (Cross-docking).
3. **Branding:** The "Value" destination for millions of families.
4. **Counter-Positioning:** Historically counter-positioned against downtown department stores by building in rural areas with massive footprints and lower costs.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Sam Walton Era:** Explosive growth through rural dominance.
- **The E-commerce Pivot:** Integration of Jet.com and massive tech investment to fight Amazon.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Labor Costs:** Upward pressure on minimum wages.
- **Amazon Competition:** Continued erosion of general merchandise margins.
- **International Execution:** Struggled in some markets (e.g., Germany, Japan).

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Owned WMT for years, admiring the "low cost provider" status, though he eventually sold in favor of Apple/Amazon.
- **Sam Walton:** The ultimate "Architect" of the retail flywheel.
