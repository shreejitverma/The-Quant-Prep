# Stock: Nestlé (NESN.SW)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Nestlé is the world's largest food and beverage company. It operates a "house of brands" strategy with over 2,000 brands (Nespresso, Maggi, KitKat, Purina). It is the ultimate defensive play, offering stable growth, reliable dividends, and a massive footprint in emerging markets.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Brand:** A portfolio of "Power Brands" that command premium shelf space and pricing power globally.
*   **Scale Economies:** Massive procurement power and a global distribution network that reaches the smallest village shops.
*   **Cornered Resource:** Dominant position in specialized categories like PetCare (Purina) and Coffee (Nespresso/Starbucks at-home).
*   **Process Power:** Exceptional R&D in food science and nutritional health.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **The "Swiss Bond":** Known for extreme stability; often used by investors as a higher-yielding alternative to government bonds.
*   **CAGR:** Historically delivers consistent mid-single-digit organic growth plus dividends.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Health Trends:** Shift away from processed foods and sugar (GLP-1 impact).
*   **Input Costs:** Inflation in coffee, cocoa, and packaging materials.
*   **Currency:** The "Swiss Franc" (CHF) strength can be a headwind for global earnings.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Buffett:** Nestlé has the "pricing power" he loves. It's the See's Candies of the world on a massive scale.
*   **Lynch:** A "Stalwart" that belongs in every conservative portfolio.
