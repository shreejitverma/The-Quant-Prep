# Stock: Danaher (DHR)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Danaher is a "company that builds companies." It is a life sciences and diagnostics giant that is famous for the **Danaher Business System (DBS)** - a relentless application of Kaizen (continuous improvement) to every business it acquires. It focuses on "razor-and-blade" models where it sells a machine and then earns high-margin recurring revenue from the consumables (tests, reagents).

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Switching Costs:** Once a lab or hospital installs a Danaher diagnostic system (e.g., Beckman Coulter, Cepheid), switching to another provider is incredibly disruptive and expensive.
2. **Process Power (DBS):** The Danaher Business System is a unique operational edge that allows them to extract more margin from acquired businesses than anyone else.
3. **Scale Economies:** Global distribution and R&D in life sciences.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Rales Era:** Transition from a real estate trust to an industrial conglomerate, and finally to a healthcare/life sciences powerhouse.
- **CAGR:** One of the best performing stocks in the S&P 500 over the last 30 years.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Post-Pandemic Slump:** Declining COVID testing revenue (Cepheid).
- **M&A Risk:** Overpaying for a large acquisition.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Charlie Munger:** Famously admired Danaher, calling it a "world-class" example of how to run a conglomerate.
- **Warren Buffett:** Likely views Danaher as a "best-in-class" operator similar to Berkshire's own subsidiaries.
