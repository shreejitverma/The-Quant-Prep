# Stock: UnitedHealth Group (UNH)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
UnitedHealth is the dominant force in US healthcare. It operates two distinct flywheels: UnitedHealthcare (the largest private insurer) and Optum (a massive health services business). By owning both the insurer and the provider (Optum), UNH can manage costs more effectively than any competitor, capturing margin at every step of the patient journey.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Largest data set and purchasing power in the US healthcare system.
2. **Switching Costs:** Corporate health plans are sticky; switching carriers is a major administrative burden for HR departments.
3. **Process Power:** Optum's data analytics and pharmacy benefit management (PBM) create efficiencies that smaller insurers cannot match.
4. **Network Economies:** More providers in the UNH network attract more members, which in turn attracts more providers.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The ACA Era:** UNH thrived under the Affordable Care Act by scaling its managed care models.
- **CAGR:** Massive outperformance over the last 20 years, often delivering 15-20% annualized returns.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Political Risk (Anti-Model: The Stroke of a Pen):** "Medicare for All" or major regulatory changes to PBMs or private insurance.
- **Cybersecurity:** Recent Change Healthcare breach showed the systemic risk UNH poses to the US medical system.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Admires the "managed care" model and the massive cash flow.
- **Charlie Munger:** Respected the operational efficiency and the "flywheel" between insurance and services.
