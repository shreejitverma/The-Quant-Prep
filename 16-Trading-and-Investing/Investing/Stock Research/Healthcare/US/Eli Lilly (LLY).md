# Stock: Eli Lilly (LLY)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Eli Lilly has transformed into the most valuable pharmaceutical company in the world, primarily due to its leadership in metabolic health (GLP-1 drugs for diabetes and obesity). With Mounjaro and Zepbound, Lilly is addressing what could be the largest pharmaceutical market in history.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Cornered Resource:** Proprietary GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist technology and patents.
2. **Scale Economies:** Massive manufacturing investments to meet the global demand for weight-loss injections.
3. **Branding:** Dominance in the "Diabetes" mindshare for over a century (one of the first to mass-produce insulin).

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Insulin Era:** Decades of steady growth.
- **The GLP-1 Era (2022-Present):** Parabolic stock growth as clinical data for Tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound) showed unprecedented weight loss.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Manufacturing Bottlenecks:** Inability to produce enough product to meet demand.
- **Safety Signals:** Long-term side effects of GLP-1s could emerge.
- **Pricing Pressure:** Government negotiation of drug prices (IRA).

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Peter Lynch:** Would love the "story" here - a drug that millions of people need and will likely take for many years.
- **Warren Buffett:** Generally avoids high-growth pharma, but would appreciate the "essentiality" of the products.
