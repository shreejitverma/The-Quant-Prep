# Stock: Roche (ROG.SW)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Roche is a global leader in Oncology (cancer) and Diagnostics. It is unique among big pharma for its integrated "Diagnostics + Pharma" model, enabling personalized healthcare. Roche is a R&D powerhouse, consistently reinvesting a higher percentage of sales into its pipeline than most peers.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Cornered Resource:** Unrivaled portfolio of cancer drugs and a dominant position in the global diagnostics market (lab testing).
*   **Switching Costs:** High; hospitals and labs globally are locked into Roche's proprietary testing machines and reagents.
*   **Scale Economies:** Massive R&D engine and global clinical trial infrastructure.
*   **Process Power:** Decades of expertise in complex biologics manufacturing.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Returns:** Strong history of dividend growth (Swiss Dividend Aristocrat).
*   **Innovation Cycle:** Performance is often tied to the success/failure of major phase-3 clinical trials.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Patent Cliffs:** Competition from "biosimilars" as older blockbuster drugs lose patent protection.
*   **R&D Risk:** The inherent uncertainty of drug development.
*   **US Healthcare Policy:** Potential for drug price negotiations in the US market.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Lynch:** A "Stalwart" with "Fast Grower" optionality in its pipeline.
*   **Munger:** Would admire the family-controlled stability (Hoffmann-La Roche families) and the focus on "deep science."
