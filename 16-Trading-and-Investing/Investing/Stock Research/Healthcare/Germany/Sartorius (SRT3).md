# Stock: Sartorius (SRT3)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
Mission-critical supplier to the biopharma industry. Dominant in 'single-use' technologies for biologic drug manufacturing.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Switching Costs: Highly regulated manufacturing processes make changing suppliers difficult. Process Power: High-tech membrane and filtration technology.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Explosive growth driven by the rise of biologics and biosimilars.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Post-pandemic inventory destocking; high valuation multiples.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Lynch: 'Selling the picks and shovels for the biotech gold rush.'
