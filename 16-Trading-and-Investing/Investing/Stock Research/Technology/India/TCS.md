# Stock: TCS (TCS)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is the crown jewel of the Tata Group and a global leader in IT services. It is the primary beneficiary of the "Cloud and AI" transition for Fortune 500 companies. TCS operates with best-in-class margins and an asset-light model that generates enormous free cash flow.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Switching Costs:** High; enterprise clients (banks, retailers) cannot easily migrate their legacy and mission-critical systems.
*   **Scale Economies:** Unmatched talent pool (>600k employees) allows for competitive bidding on massive multi-year transformation deals.
*   **Brand:** The "Tata" name provides instant global credibility and trust.
*   **Process Power:** Rigorous execution and "Machine First" delivery model (MFDM).

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Returns:** High double-digit ROE (>40%) and ROIC. Consistently outperforms the Nifty IT index.
*   **Dividends:** High payout ratio; a reliable "cash cow" for shareholders.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Visa Regulations:** US H1-B visa policy changes.
*   **AI Disruption:** The risk of GenAI automating entry-level coding/testing, requiring a pivot to higher-value consulting.
*   **Currency:** Sensitivity to USD/INR fluctuations.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Buffett:** A high-quality "Capital Light" business.
*   **Lynch:** A "Stalwart" that is essential to the global economy.
