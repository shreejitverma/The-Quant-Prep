# Stock: TSMC (TSM)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
The world's most important company. The sole provider of cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Process Power: Unmatched manufacturing precision. Scale Economies: Massive CapEx requirements create a high barrier. Network Effects: Ecosystem of design tools and customers.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Central to the AI and mobile revolutions; dominant market share.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Geopolitical risk (Taiwan Strait); concentration of global tech on one island.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Munger: 'A tech moat that is almost impossible to cross.'
