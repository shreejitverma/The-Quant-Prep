# Stock: Nemetschek (NEM)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
European leader in AEC (Architecture, Engineering, Construction) software. Strong position in BIM (Building Information Modeling).

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Network Effects: Industry standard for collaboration in construction projects. Switching Costs: High learning curve and integration into workflows.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Transitioning to SaaS; consistent high margins and organic growth.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Cyclicality of the construction industry; competition from Autodesk.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Munger: 'Software is a great business once you are the standard.'
