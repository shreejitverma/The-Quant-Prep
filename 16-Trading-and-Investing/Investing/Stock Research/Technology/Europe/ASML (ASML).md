# Stock: ASML (ASML)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
ASML is a literal monopoly in the most important technology on earth: EUV (Extreme Ultraviolet) Lithography. Without ASML's machines, it is physically impossible to manufacture the most advanced chips (used in iPhones, AI servers, and defense). It is the ultimate bottleneck and "choke point" of the global technology supply chain.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Cornered Resource:** ASML is the *only* company in the world capable of producing EUV lithography machines.
2. **Scale Economies:** The R&D required to build these machines (tens of billions) and the complexity of the supply chain (1,000+ specialized vendors) create an insurmountable barrier to entry.
3. **Switching Costs:** TSMC, Intel, and Samsung build their entire billion-dollar fabs around ASML's specifications.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **Pre-EUV:** Steady growth in DUV (Deep Ultraviolet).
- **EUV Era (2018-Present):** Massive expansion in margins and valuation as EUV became the industry standard for sub-7nm nodes.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Geopolitics:** Trade wars between US and China; ASML is often forbidden from selling advanced gear to China.
- **Single-Point Failure:** Highly complex supply chain (e.g., Zeiss lenses) means any disruption stops production.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Charlie Munger:** Would love this "Lollapalooza" of technical complexity, monopoly power, and essentiality.
- **Peter Lynch:** A "monopoly in a niche" is Lynch's favorite kind of company.
