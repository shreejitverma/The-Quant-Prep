# Stock: Sony Group (6758)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Sony has successfully transformed from a struggling electronics hardware company into a global entertainment powerhouse. It is now a unique "Creative Entertainment" conglomerate with dominant positions in Gaming (PlayStation), Music (largest publisher), Pictures (Spider-Man, etc.), and Image Sensors (iPhone supplier).

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Network Effects:** PlayStation Network (PSN) creates a locked-in ecosystem of 100M+ active users.
*   **Cornered Resource:** Invaluable IP (Music catalog, movie franchises) and world-leading CMOS sensor technology.
*   **Switching Costs:** Extremely high within the PlayStation ecosystem (digital library, friend lists).
*   **Scale Economies:** Dominance in CMOS sensors allows for higher R&D spend that competitors cannot match.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Recovery:** Since the 2012 turnaround, Sony has significantly outperformed the Nikkei 225.
*   **Profitability:** Shifted from high-volume/low-margin hardware to high-margin recurring services and IP.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Cloud Gaming:** Potential disruption of the console model by services like Xbox Cloud or Netflix Gaming.
*   **Hardware Cycles:** Dependence on the multi-year PlayStation hardware refresh cycle.
*   **Geopolitics:** Tensions affecting the global semiconductor supply chain.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Lynch:** A "Turnaround" that became a "Fast Grower" in its digital segments.
*   **Buffett:** Might appreciate the music publishing business - it's essentially a "royalty on human emotion."
