# Stock: NVIDIA (NVDA)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
NVIDIA has evolved from a gaming GPU company into the "arms dealer" of the AI era. Its accelerated computing platform (hardware + CUDA software) is the foundational layer for all LLMs (Large Language Models), autonomous vehicles, and industrial digital twins. It owns the "Full Stack" of AI compute.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Cornered Resource:** Proprietary GPU architecture (H100/Blackwell) and the CUDA software ecosystem.
2. **Switching Costs:** Developers are trained on CUDA. Moving to another hardware provider requires rewriting massive amounts of code, creating a powerful software moat around hardware.
3. **Network Economies:** The more developers use CUDA, the more software is optimized for NVIDIA, making NVIDIA hardware more attractive.
4. **Scale Economies:** Massive R&D lead that competitors (AMD, Intel) struggle to match.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **Gaming Era:** Dominance in PC gaming.
- **Crypto Era:** GPUs used for mining (volatile period).
- **AI Era (2022-Present):** Exponential growth following the ChatGPT moment.
- **CAGR:** One of the best performing stocks of the last decade, with massive multi-bagger returns.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Geopolitics (Anti-Model: The Fortress):** China export restrictions.
- **Cyclicality:** Semiconductor industry is historically cyclical.
- **In-house Silicon:** Major customers (Google, Amazon, Microsoft) developing their own AI chips (TPUs, Trainium).

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Jensen Huang:** The visionary founder-CEO whom Munger and Buffett would likely admire for his "long-term thinking" and ability to "skate to where the puck is going."
- **Peter Lynch:** Would have caught this early by seeing kids obsessed with gaming PCs.
