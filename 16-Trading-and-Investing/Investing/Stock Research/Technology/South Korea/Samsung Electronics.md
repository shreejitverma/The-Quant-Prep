# Stock: Samsung Electronics (005930)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
A vertically integrated tech titan. Leader in Memory (DRAM/NAND) and high-end OLED displays.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Scale Economies: Massive production scale in chips and screens. Process Power: R&D leadership in semiconductor manufacturing.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Cyclical but dominant; a core play on global tech hardware.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Memory price volatility; competition from TSMC and Chinese chipmakers.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Buffett: 'A tough, cyclical, but ultimately dominant industrial giant.'
