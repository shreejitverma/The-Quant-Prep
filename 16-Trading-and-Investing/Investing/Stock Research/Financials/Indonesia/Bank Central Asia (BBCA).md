# Stock: Bank Central Asia (BBCA)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
Indonesia's premier private bank. Known for its low-cost CASA (Current Account Savings Account) base and superior technology.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Scale Economies: Dominant payment system in Indonesia. Switching Costs: The primary transaction bank for Indonesian businesses.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
One of the most profitable banks globally with high ROE and low NPLs.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Indonesian macro-economic volatility; fintech competition.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Munger: 'The low-cost producer in banking is a winner.'
