# Stock: HDFC Bank (HDFCBANK)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
HDFC Bank is the gold standard of Indian banking, known for its conservative lending, superior asset quality, and relentless focus on retail credit. Following its merger with parent HDFC Ltd, it is now a top-10 global bank by market cap. It serves as the backbone of the Indian financial system and a primary vehicle for FII (Foreign Institutional Investor) participation in India.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Brand:** Synonymous with trust and stability in the Indian retail mindspace.
*   **Switching Costs:** Deep integration into customer lives through salary accounts, mortgages, and credit cards.
*   **Scale Economies:** Lowest cost of funds (CASA ratio) due to its massive branch network and digital dominance.
*   **Process Power:** Exceptionally low NPA (Non-Performing Asset) levels maintained over decades through rigorous underwriting.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **CAGR (20Y):** Consistently delivered ~20% earnings growth for two decades, significantly outperforming the Bank Nifty.
*   **Post-Merger Volatility:** Currently undergoing a "digestion" phase post-merger, leading to temporary NIM (Net Interest Margin) compression.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Merger Integration:** Balancing the higher cost of liabilities from the mortgage business.
*   **Leadership Transition:** Moving beyond the era of Aditya Puri; maintaining the "HDFC Culture."
*   **Fintech Disruption:** Competition from UPI-based payment apps and neo-banks.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Buffett:** The "moat" is the low cost of capital and the "brand" of safety.
*   **Munger:** An example of "Lollapalooza" effects where culture, scale, and technology converge.
