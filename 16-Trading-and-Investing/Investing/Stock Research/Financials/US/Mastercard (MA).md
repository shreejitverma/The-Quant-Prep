# Stock: Mastercard (MA)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Nearly identical to Visa, Mastercard operates as the other half of the global payment duopoly. While slightly smaller than Visa, it is often seen as more "innovative" in value-added services (cybersecurity, data analytics), which helps drive higher yield per transaction.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Network Economies:** Global acceptance network that is virtually impossible for a newcomer to replicate.
2. **Scale Economies:** Massive volume allows for the highest possible security and reliability spend.
3. **Switching Costs:** Integrated into billions of digital wallets and merchant systems.
4. **Branding:** "Priceless" campaign created one of the most recognizable financial brands.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **IPO to Present:** Has historically outperformed Visa in terms of percentage growth due to its smaller starting base and aggressive international push.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Anti-Trust:** Constant legal battles over fee structures.
- **Nationalism:** Countries like China (UnionPay) or Russia developing their own internal payment rails for "sovereignty."

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Views the V/MA duopoly as a "wonderful business" because it doesn't require capital to grow - it just collects fees on other people's spending.
