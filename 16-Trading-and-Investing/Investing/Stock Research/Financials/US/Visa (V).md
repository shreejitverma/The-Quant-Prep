# Stock: Visa (V)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Visa is not a bank; it is a global data network. It sits in the middle of nearly every digital transaction on earth, taking a small "toll" on the movement of money. It benefits from the secular trend of "Cash to Digital" and has extremely high margins because the cost of processing an additional transaction is nearly zero.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Network Economies:** The classic "Two-Sided Network." Merchants accept Visa because everyone has a card; consumers carry a Visa because every merchant accepts it.
2. **Branding:** Instant trust globally.
3. **Scale Economies:** Fixed costs are high, but marginal costs are zero, leading to massive operating leverage.
4. **Switching Costs:** Once a merchant or bank is integrated into the Visa Net, moving to a new standard is incredibly difficult.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **2008 IPO:** One of the most successful IPOs in history.
- **Consistent Double-Digit Growth:** Driven by e-commerce and international expansion.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Regulation:** Caps on "swipe fees" (Interchange).
- **Alternative Rails:** FedNow, Pix (Brazil), UPI (India), and Crypto/Stablecoins bypassing the Visa network.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Owns both V and MA. He loves the "toll bridge" business model.
- **Charlie Munger:** Admired the "unavoidable" nature of the payment networks.
