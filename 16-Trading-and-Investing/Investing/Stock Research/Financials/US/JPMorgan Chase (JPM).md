# Stock: JPMorgan Chase (JPM)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
JPMorgan is the "Fortress Balance Sheet" of the banking world. It has successfully navigated every crisis of the last century, often emerging stronger by acquiring distressed rivals. Its scale allows it to outspend any other bank on technology ($15B+ annually), creating a widening gap between it and smaller regional banks.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Massive tech budget and branch network.
2. **Switching Costs:** Personal and business banking customers rarely switch due to the "hassle" of moving direct deposits, bill pays, and integrated treasury services.
3. **Branding:** A symbol of stability in the global financial system.
4. **Network Economies:** Dominance in investment banking and market-making creates a liquidity pool that attracts more clients.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **2008 Crisis:** Acquired Bear Stearns and Washington Mutual, solidifying its "Fortress" status.
- **Jamie Dimon Era:** Consistently higher ROE (Return on Equity) than peers.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Macroeconomic Shock:** Severe global recession or credit default cycle.
- **Regulation:** Increased capital requirements or "too big to fail" break-up pressure.
- **Fintech Disruption:** Neobanks and DeFi eroding the retail deposit base.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Warren Buffett:** Has long praised Jamie Dimon's annual letters and JPM's operational excellence (though he sold much of his bank holdings recently).
- **Jamie Dimon:** The ultimate "Architect" of the modern banking conglomerate.
