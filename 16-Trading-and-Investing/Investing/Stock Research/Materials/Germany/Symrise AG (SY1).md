# Stock: Symrise AG (SY1)

status:: deep-research
priority:: high

## Investment Thesis
Global leader in flavors and fragrances. Highly defensive industry with high barriers to entry and sticky customer relationships.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
Switching Costs: Flavors are a tiny part of end product cost but critical for taste. Scale Economies: Global R&D and supply chain.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, long-term context)
Steady 5-7% organic growth supplemented by strategic M&A.

## Risk Factors (Anti-Models)
Raw material price volatility; regulatory changes in chemical safety.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
Buffett would appreciate the 'toll bridge' nature of flavors in consumer staples.
