# Stock: Hermès (RMS.PA)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Hermès is the "pinnacle of luxury." Unlike conglomerates like LVMH, Hermès focuses on extreme scarcity and craftsmanship. Its "Birkin" and "Kelly" bags are not just products; they are considered alternative asset classes with better historical returns than many equities.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Brand:** The most prestigious brand in the world, built on 180+ years of heritage and "anti-marketing."
*   **Cornered Resource:** Exclusive access to the highest quality leathers and a limited pool of master artisans in France.
*   **Pricing Power:** Incredible; Hermès can raise prices significantly without impacting demand (Veblen Good).
*   **Counter-Positioning:** Avoiding mass-market trends and "logo-mania" to maintain exclusivity.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Outperformance:** Massive outperformance vs the CAC 40 and even most other luxury peers.
*   **Resilience:** Historically less sensitive to economic downturns than "aspirational luxury" brands.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Key Person Risk:** Dependence on the creative direction and the Hermès family leadership.
*   **Supply Constraints:** Scarcity is a moat, but it also limits the absolute ceiling for growth.
*   **China Exposure:** High sensitivity to the Chinese luxury consumer.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Buffett:** Would love the "untouchable" brand and the lack of inventory risk (everything sells).
*   **Munger:** "Hermès is a business where the product sells itself because it is the best."
