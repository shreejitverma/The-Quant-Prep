# Stock: LVMH (MC.PA)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
LVMH (Louis Vuitton Moët Hennessy) is the undisputed king of luxury. It is a house of "heritage" brands (Louis Vuitton, Dior, Tiffany, Dom Pérignon). Luxury goods have a negative price elasticity (Veblen goods) - the more expensive they are, the more desirable they become. LVMH is the ultimate play on the "Rising Global Wealth" theme.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Branding:** Centuries-old heritage that cannot be "bought" or "coded." It must be earned over time.
2. **Cornered Resource:** The "Maîtres" (Master Artisans) and the exclusive locations in luxury districts.
3. **Scale Economies:** LVMH can buy the best real estate and the best talent across its 75 "Maisons," sharing back-office costs while maintaining brand independence.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Bernard Arnault Era:** Consolidating the fragmented luxury market into a single powerhouse.
- **The Asian Boom:** Riding the wave of the Chinese middle and upper class.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Geopolitics:** Economic slowdown in China.
- **Counterfeiting:** Erosion of brand exclusivity.
- **Succession:** Transition from the Arnault family.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Bernard Arnault:** The "Wolf in Cashmere" - a ruthless acquirer and master brand-builder whom Munger would likely admire for his discipline.
- **Warren Buffett:** Loves companies with "pricing power," and LVMH has it in spades.
