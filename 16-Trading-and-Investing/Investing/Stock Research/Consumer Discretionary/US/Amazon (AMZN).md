# Stock: Amazon (AMZN)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Amazon is the "Everything Store" and the world's largest cloud provider (AWS). It operates on a "Day 1" philosophy of relentless customer obsession. Its retail side is a massive logistics flywheel that lowers costs and increases speed, while its AWS side provides high-margin cash flow to fund further experimentation (AI, Prime Video, Kuiper).

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Fulfillment network and AWS infrastructure are so large that Amazon's unit costs are significantly lower than any competitor.
2. **Switching Costs:** Prime membership (for retail) and AWS (for developers/enterprises).
3. **Network Economies:** The Amazon Marketplace. More sellers attract more buyers, which attracts more sellers.
4. **Counter-Positioning:** AWS counter-positioned against IBM/Oracle by offering pay-as-you-go pricing for startups.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Book Era:** Disruption of traditional retail.
- **The AWS Era:** Discovery of the high-margin cloud engine.
- **The Advertising Era:** Massive recent growth in high-margin retail media.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Antitrust:** Potential for a breakup of Retail and AWS.
- **AWS Competition:** Azure and GCP gaining ground.
- **Logistics Complexity:** Increasing costs of the "Last Mile."

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Jeff Bezos:** The ultimate "Architect" of the 21st-century conglomerate.
- **Warren Buffett:** Admitted he was "an idiot" for missing Amazon early, but his team eventually bought in.
