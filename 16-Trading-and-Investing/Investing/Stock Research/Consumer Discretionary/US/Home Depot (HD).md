# Stock: Home Depot (HD)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Home Depot is the "Fortress" of the home improvement industry. It focuses on the high-value "Pro" customer (contractors, plumbers, electricians), who are more frequent and higher-spending than average DIYers. Its massive store footprint acts as a "hub-and-spoke" for local construction, making it immune to many of the disruption factors that hit general retail.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Scale Economies:** Purchasing power over suppliers and massive distribution efficiency.
2. **Switching Costs:** Pros build their businesses around Home Depot's "ProXtra" rewards and supply chain.
3. **Branding:** The "Orange Apron" culture of expertise and reliability.
4. **Location/Real Estate:** Prime locations that would be impossible to replicate today.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **1980s-90s:** Rapid expansion of the big-box format.
- **Post-2008:** Shift from store growth to "One Home Depot" (omnichannel and Pro focus).

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Housing Market:** Highly sensitive to mortgage rates and home turnover.
- **Labor:** Shortage of skilled retail labor.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Peter Lynch:** A quintessential "Invest in what you know" stock; easy to see the parking lots are always full.
- **Warren Buffett:** Loves the "essential" nature of the category.
