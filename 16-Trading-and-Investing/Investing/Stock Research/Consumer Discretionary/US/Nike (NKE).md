# Stock: Nike (NKE)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
Nike is the premier storyteller in sports. It has successfully moved from a footwear company to a global fitness brand. Its "Direct-to-Consumer" (DTC) shift has allowed it to recapture margin and own the relationship with its fans via the Nike and SNKRS apps.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Branding:** The "Swoosh" and the "Just Do It" slogan represent a massive emotional moat.
2. **Cornered Resource:** Endorsement contracts with the world's top athletes (Jordan, LeBron, Ronaldo).
3. **Scale Economies:** Massive R&D for performance tech (Air, Flyknit) and global marketing spend.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Jordan Era:** Turning sneakers into a cultural asset.
- **The Digital Pivot:** Focus on apps and first-party data.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Inventory Cycles:** Excess stock leading to discounting.
- **Competition:** Hoka, On, and Lululemon taking share in specific niches.
- **Social Media:** Brands can be "cancelled" or fall out of favor more quickly than in the past.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Phil Knight:** The founder-architect who built the brand on "emotion" rather than just "rubber."
- **Peter Lynch:** A classic example of a brand you see everywhere.
