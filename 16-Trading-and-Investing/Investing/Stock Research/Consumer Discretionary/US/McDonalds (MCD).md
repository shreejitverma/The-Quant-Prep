# Stock: McDonalds (MCD)

## Investment Thesis (Why it is a consistent compounder)
McDonald's is a real estate company that happens to sell burgers. It owns the land and buildings of its franchisees, collecting high-margin rent and royalties. This "asset-heavy in land, asset-light in operations" model provides incredible stability and inflation protection.

## Financial Moat (Connect to Hamilton Helmer's 7 Powers)
1. **Cornered Resource (Real Estate):** Thousands of the best "Corner" locations in the world.
2. **Branding:** The Golden Arches are a global symbol of consistency and value.
3. **Scale Economies:** Massive purchasing power for ingredients, allowing them to underprice almost any competitor.
4. **Process Power:** The "Speedy System" - the gold standard for fast-food operations.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, key eras)
- **The Kroc Era:** Scaling through franchising.
- **The "Experience of the Future":** Digital kiosks, delivery, and a modernized menu.

## Risk Factors (Connect to Anti-Models)
- **Health Trends:** Increased scrutiny on "Ultra-Processed Foods" (UPF).
- **Labor Costs:** Automation is the primary defense against rising minimum wages.

## Architect Perspective (What would Buffett/Munger/Lynch say?)
- **Ray Kroc:** The "Architect" who turned a burger stand into a global empire.
- **Warren Buffett:** A regular customer and admirer of the franchising model.
