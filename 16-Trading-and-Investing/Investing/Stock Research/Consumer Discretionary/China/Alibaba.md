# Stock: Alibaba (BABA)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
Alibaba is the "Amazon of China," but with a higher-margin marketplace model (Taobao, Tmall) and a dominant cloud business (Alibaba Cloud). Despite recent regulatory headwinds and increased competition from PDD and ByteDance, Alibaba remains a core infrastructure player in the Chinese economy.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Network Effects:** The massive buyer-seller ecosystem on Tmall and Taobao creates a powerful flywheel.
*   **Scale Economies:** Unmatched logistics network (Cainiao) and data processing capabilities.
*   **Switching Costs:** High for cloud customers and merchants integrated into the Alibaba marketing/analytics suite.
*   **Brand:** Still the go-to destination for global brands (Apple, Estee Lauder) to reach Chinese consumers.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Volatility:** Significant underperformance since 2020 due to the Ant Group IPO cancellation and regulatory fines.
*   **Value Play:** Currently trading at historically low valuations, suggesting a "Turnaround" or "Deep Value" opportunity.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Competition:** Fierce price wars with PDD (Pinduoduo) and the rise of "Live-stream e-commerce" (TikTok/Douyin).
*   **Regulatory Risk:** The "Common Prosperity" agenda and antitrust monitoring.
*   **Geopolitics:** US-China trade tensions and audit compliance.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Munger:** FAMOUSLY invested in BABA (via Daily Journal), later admitting he underestimated the competitive threat from PDD.
*   **Lynch:** A "Turnaround" play where the "Asset Play" (Cloud, Cainiao, International) might be worth more than the current market cap.
