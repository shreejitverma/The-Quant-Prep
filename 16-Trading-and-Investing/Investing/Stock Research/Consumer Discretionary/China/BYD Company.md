# Stock: BYD Company (1211.HK)

## Investment Thesis (Global & Regional Context)
BYD (Build Your Dreams) is the world's leading manufacturer of electric vehicles (EVs) and plug-in hybrids. Unlike Tesla, BYD is highly vertically integrated, manufacturing its own batteries (Blade Battery) and semiconductors. It is the primary engine of China's "New Energy Vehicle" global expansion.

## Financial Moat (7 Powers Analysis)
*   **Cornered Resource:** In-house battery technology (LFP Blade Battery) is considered world-class and is even supplied to competitors (like Tesla).
*   **Scale Economies:** Massive volume in China allows for a price point that Western manufacturers cannot currently match.
*   **Process Power:** Extreme vertical integration (making almost everything except tires and glass) provides superior margin control.
*   **Counter-Positioning:** Offering a wide range of affordable EVs and Hybrids while legacy makers were focused on luxury BEVs.

## Historical Performance (CAGR, Local Market Index Comparison)
*   **Outperformance:** Significant outperformer in the EV space over the last 5 years.
*   **Profitability:** One of the few EV makers consistently generating substantial profits.

## Risk Factors (Currency, Geopolitical, Sector)
*   **Tariffs:** Protectionist measures from the EU and US against Chinese EV imports.
*   **Price Wars:** Intense domestic competition in China leading to margin compression.
*   **Subsidies:** Phasing out of Chinese government EV subsidies.

## Architect Perspective (Buffett/Munger/Lynch)
*   **Buffett:** Berkshire Hathaway's investment in BYD (prompted by Charlie Munger) is one of their most successful tech bets.
*   **Munger:** "I have never seen anything like BYD. Wang Chuanfu is a combination of Thomas Edison and Jack Welch."
