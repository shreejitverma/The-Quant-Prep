## 1. Executive Summary & Variant Perception

## 2. Business Quality & Competitive Moat (7 Powers)
- **Scale Economies**: Does unit cost decline systematically with scale?
- **Network Effects**: Does the value of the platform increase with each incremental user?
- **Switching Costs**: What operational, procedural, or financial friction prevents customer defection?
- **Branding / Pricing Power**: Can the company raise prices above inflation without losing volume?
- **Cornered Resource / Process Power**: Proprietary technology, exclusive patents, or organizational secret sauce.

## 3. Financial Auditing & Forensic Accounting
- **Revenue Quality**: Organic versus acquisition-driven growth, customer concentration, and recurring ARR versus transactional revenue.
- **Unit Economics & Margin Structure**: Gross margin, operating margin, and incremental operating margin expansion.
- **Forensic Health Checks**:
  - Beneish M-Score: Detection of earnings manipulation or aggressive revenue recognition.
  - Altman Z-Score: Balance sheet solvency and bankruptcy distance.
  - Cash Conversion Cycle & Working Capital: Free cash flow conversion ratio ($\text{FCF} / \text{Net Income}$).
- **Capital Allocation Track Record**: 10-year ROIC vs WACC spread, buyback timing efficacy, and M&A discipline.
