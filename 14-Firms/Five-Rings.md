# Five Rings

> [!info] Draft
> Facts below were gathered from the sources listed at the end on 2026-09-28.
> Firms change their processes often; verify anything that matters with your recruiter.
> Five Rings publishes little about itself, so more of this guide rests on candidate reports than usual.

## Snapshot

- Five Rings is a proprietary trading firm that trades "in a multitude of exciting markets, established and esoteric, domestic and international".
- It says it takes strategies from concept to implementation within days or weeks.
- Offices are in New York and London.
- It describes itself as a team-first meritocracy and takes its name and mottos from Miyamoto Musashi's The Book of Five Rings.
- Founding year, headcount and products traded are not published on the pages consulted.

## Roles

- Quantitative trader: analyses the risks and rewards of markets, explores new strategies, and works with researchers and developers to build trading models.
- Quantitative researcher: develops trading models and strategies; Five Rings lists Python, R and advanced mathematics as required.
- Software developer: builds trading software in C++ on Linux in a distributed system, covering strategy implementation, exchange connectivity, trader tools and model translation.
- Internships run for trading, research and development in the summer, plus a January externship; trading interns need strong quantitative skills and basic software development ability.

## Interview process

> [!warning] Commonly reported, verify with your recruiter
> Round counts and formats vary by role, office and year.
> Five Rings does not publish its process; everything below is from candidate reports.

- Trading candidates commonly report an early screen of rapid-fire mental arithmetic and estimation, each question on a short timer, which many describe as the main filter.
- Later technical calls reportedly focus on probability, expected value and game theory, often building from a base question to a twist and then a generalisation.
- The final round is reportedly a long day, on site or by video, meeting traders, researchers and developers.
- Developer candidates report C++ and algorithm questions; researcher candidates report probability, statistics and coding.

## Preparation plan

1. Week 1: run `./qp firms five-rings`; start `./qp drill arith` and `./qp drill optiver` daily and log scores with `./qp stats`.
2. Week 2: [Estimation and Fermi Problems](../13-Interview-Playbook/04-Estimation-and-Fermi-Problems.md) under a 30-second budget, plus [Conditional Probability and Bayes](../01-Probability/02-Conditional-Probability-and-Bayes.md).
3. Week 3: [Classic Expectation Problems](../01-Probability/14-Classic-Expectation-Problems.md) and [Markov Chains](../01-Probability/10-Markov-Chains.md); after each problem, ask yourself the twist and the generalisation.
4. Week 4: [Game Theory for Traders](../11-Risk-and-Trading/07-Game-Theory-for-Traders.md) and [Trading Games in Interviews](../13-Interview-Playbook/05-Trading-Games-in-Interviews.md), with `./qp drill mm`.
5. Week 5: mock interviews with a partner who pushes follow-ups; developers add timed C++ practice; clear `./qp review --track quant-trader`.

## Culture and what they value

- Five Rings describes "building a superior team and an open and fun culture", with a strong emphasis on mentorship.
- It calls itself a team-first meritocracy that values constant innovation.
- Its quoted mottos, "It is essential to keep your mind free from subjective biases" and "Become aware of what is not obvious", signal the reasoning style it wants.

## Senior hire notes

- The positions page lists full-time roles without a separate experienced-hire track; ask the recruiter whether the speed screens apply to you.
- Experienced traders and developers should expect the same probability and problem-solving bar plus detailed discussion of their own work.
- Keep a prior employer's proprietary strategies and code out of the conversation.
- Non-compete and garden-leave terms vary by jurisdiction; check your contract before agreeing a start date.
- See [Senior-Level Expectations](../13-Interview-Playbook/12-Senior-Level-Expectations.md).
