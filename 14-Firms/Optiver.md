# Optiver

> [!info] Draft
> Facts below were gathered from the sources listed at the end on 2026-09-27.
> Firms change their processes often; verify anything that matters with your recruiter.

## Snapshot

- Optiver is a market maker: it continuously quotes buy and sell prices across financial markets using its own capital and technology.
- It is widely known as an options market maker; its own pages describe liquidity provision "across products, venues and market conditions".
- Amsterdam is the historical home office; the firm marked its 40th anniversary in 2025.
- Offices listed on its site: Amsterdam, Austin, Chicago, Hong Kong, London, Mumbai, New York, Shanghai, Singapore, Sydney and Taipei.
- Headcount is not published on the pages consulted.

## Roles

- Trader: quotes and manages risk in listed products, mostly derivatives, and improves the strategies behind the quotes.
- Quantitative researcher: per a 2027 graduate listing, builds models for pricing, forecasting and signal discovery, analyses large datasets, and monitors models in production.
- Software engineer and FPGA engineer: build the low-latency trading systems, tooling and hardware; the firm hires graduates and interns for both.

## Interview process

> [!warning] Commonly reported, verify with your recruiter
> Round counts and formats vary by role, office and year.

- Optiver's own listings say applicants complete online assessments after applying, often within a few days of the invitation; the time limit varies by programme.
- A 2027 graduate listing states an 8-month cooldown before re-sitting Optiver graduate assessments.
- Trader candidates commonly report a timed mental arithmetic test, numerical sequences, fast probability questions and reaction or risk games as early screens.
- Later rounds commonly reported for traders include probability and options theory interviews and a market-making or trading game day.
- Researcher and engineer candidates report coding assessments, technical interviews, and for researchers a data or modelling exercise.

## Preparation plan

1. Week 1: run `./qp firms optiver`; do `./qp drill optiver` and `./qp drill arith` every day and log the scores with `./qp stats`.
2. Week 2: [Sequences and Numerical Reasoning](../13-Interview-Playbook/06-Sequences-and-Numerical-Reasoning.md) and the [Probability Problem Set](../01-Probability/15-Probability-Problem-Set.md) under a timer.
3. Week 3: options foundations, [Put-Call Parity](../05-Derivatives-and-Volatility/02-Option-Payoffs-and-Put-Call-Parity.md) then [The Greeks](../05-Derivatives-and-Volatility/05-The-Greeks.md).
4. Week 4: [Market Making Games](../08-Market-Making/10-Market-Making-Games.md) and [Options Market Making](../08-Market-Making/07-Options-Market-Making.md), with `./qp drill mm` daily.
5. Week 5: mock trading games with a partner; keep speed drills running.
6. Week 6 (engineers): [C++ for Trading Systems](../12-Quant-Development/02-Cpp-for-Trading-Systems.md) and [Low-Latency Engineering](../12-Quant-Development/03-Low-Latency-Engineering.md); everyone clears `./qp review --track quant-trader`.

## Culture and what they value

- Optiver's careers pages stress measurable progress: "prove what works, improve what comes next".
- They highlight intellectual challenge, learning at market scale and "peers who raise the bar".
- Interns are described as shadowing traders and working on real desk problems.

## Senior hire notes

- Optiver runs a separate experienced-hire pathway on its careers site.
- Experienced traders should expect questions on their product knowledge, risk management and track record, reportedly alongside the same numeric screens; confirm whether the online assessments apply to your role.
- Non-compete and garden-leave periods are common in this industry and vary by jurisdiction; check your current contract before negotiating a start date.
- See [Senior-Level Expectations](../13-Interview-Playbook/12-Senior-Level-Expectations.md).
