## Choosing where to apply

- Match the firm type to the work you want: market makers and prop firms reward fast pricing and risk decisions, HFT firms reward engineering and short-horizon research, and hedge funds reward slower, portfolio-level research.
- Traders who enjoy options and fast games will find the most overlap at [Optiver](Optiver.md), [IMC](IMC-Trading.md), [SIG](Susquehanna-SIG.md), [Akuna](Akuna-Capital.md) and [Jane Street](Jane-Street.md).
- Engineers who want low-latency C++ and systems work should look at [HRT](Hudson-River-Trading.md), [Jump](Jump-Trading.md), [Tower](Tower-Research-Capital.md), [Radix](Radix-Trading.md) and the market makers' technology teams.
- Researchers who prefer statistics, ML and portfolio construction tend to fit [Two Sigma](Two-Sigma.md), [AQR](AQR.md), [D. E. Shaw](DE-Shaw.md), [Squarepoint](Squarepoint-Capital.md) and the systematic pods at multi-manager platforms.
- On multi-manager platforms such as [Millennium](Millennium.md), [Balyasny](Balyasny.md) and Citadel, you are largely choosing a PM and a team, so evaluate the pod as carefully as the firm.
- Team-of-teams firms such as Tower also make the hiring team the main variable.
- Stability differs: pod and team roles are closer to P&L but depend on that team's results, while central and firm-wide roles are usually more stable.
- Apply to a spread of firm types if you are unsure; the core probability, statistics and coding preparation transfers across all of them.

## How the processes differ

- Speed screens are most common at options market makers and some prop firms; candidate reports describe timed arithmetic, sequences and fast probability at Optiver, IMC, Akuna, SIG and Five Rings.
- Trading games and market-making exercises appear mainly at prop firms and market makers, reportedly including Jane Street, Optiver, IMC, SIG and DRW.
- HFT firms tend to weight coding and systems knowledge heavily, and HRT publishes its own engineering interview format.
- Systematic hedge funds tend to weight statistics, ML methodology and data exercises, often with a take-home or onsite modelling task.
- Multi-manager platforms have no single process: each pod or central team runs its own, and senior hires often come through headhunters.
- Some firms say explicitly that they avoid puzzles (Tower) or that finance knowledge is not required (Jane Street, D. E. Shaw, IMC); read each firm's own guidance before preparing.
- Experienced-hire loops generally replace some screens with detailed discussion of past work, but this varies; confirm with the recruiter rather than assuming.
- For the general structure of quant interviews, see [How Quant Interviews Work](../13-Interview-Playbook/01-How-Quant-Interviews-Work.md) and [Senior-Level Expectations](../13-Interview-Playbook/12-Senior-Level-Expectations.md).
