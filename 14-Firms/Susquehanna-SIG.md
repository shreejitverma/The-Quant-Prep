# Susquehanna (SIG)

> [!info] Draft
> Facts below were gathered from the sources listed at the end on 2026-09-27.
> Firms change their processes often; verify anything that matters with your recruiter.

## Snapshot

- Susquehanna International Group (SIG) is a privately held quantitative trading firm founded in 1987.
- Wikipedia states it was founded by a group of college friends including Jeff Yass and Arthur Dantchik, and is headquartered in Bala Cynwyd, Pennsylvania.
- It makes markets and trades its own capital with a focus on derivatives, especially options, and also trades other asset classes.
- The firm states 3,500+ employees across 16+ offices.
- Other activities listed on its site include institutional sales, growth equity, venture capital and prediction markets.

## Roles

- Quantitative trader or assistant trader: makes markets and manages risk, with an extended training programme that combines classroom education and desk work.
- Quantitative research and machine learning: applies ML and quantitative research to large datasets to build trading strategies.
- Technology: builds the trading, research and data systems used across the firm.

## Interview process

> [!warning] Commonly reported, verify with your recruiter
> Round counts and formats vary by role, office and year.

- Candidate reports commonly describe a timed quantitative online test, then phone or video interviews mixing background questions with probability and maths.
- Later rounds reportedly include expected-value games or betting exercises, an interview with a senior trader, and fit conversations.
- Reports consistently stress expected-value reasoning and decision quality over memorised results.
- Research and technology loops reportedly follow a more conventional statistics or coding format.

## Preparation plan

1. Week 1: run `./qp firms susquehanna-sig`; work the [Probability Problem Set](../01-Probability/15-Probability-Problem-Set.md) and do `./qp drill arith` daily.
2. Week 2: [Expected Value and Betting Decisions](../11-Risk-and-Trading/02-Expected-Value-and-Betting-Decisions.md) and [Kelly Criterion and Position Sizing](../11-Risk-and-Trading/01-Kelly-Criterion-and-Position-Sizing.md); state the EV of every decision out loud.
3. Week 3: [Game Theory for Traders](../11-Risk-and-Trading/07-Game-Theory-for-Traders.md); play poker or other strategy games and review decisions, not outcomes.
4. Week 4: [Market Making Games](../08-Market-Making/10-Market-Making-Games.md) with `./qp drill mm`, then options basics from [The Greeks](../05-Derivatives-and-Volatility/05-The-Greeks.md).
5. Week 5: mock games with a partner and [Behavioral and Fit](../13-Interview-Playbook/09-Behavioral-and-Fit.md); clear `./qp review --track quant-trader`.

## Culture and what they value

- SIG names decision science, collaboration and game theory as core pillars.
- It uses poker and other strategy games to teach decision-making under uncertainty, per its careers pages.
- Its trader programmes split time between desk work and classroom education, including mock trading.

## Senior hire notes
